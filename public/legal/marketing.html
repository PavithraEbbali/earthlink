How we advertise

Marketing Policy

The standards we hold ourselves to in every ad, landing page, and phone call.

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Skyline Connect LLC, an authorized EarthLink retailer

Skyline Connect LLC is an independent authorized retailer of EarthLink services. We are not EarthLink LLC, and this is not the official EarthLink website.

1. Honest, non-deceptive advertising

We are committed to truthful marketing. Our advertising aims to be clear, accurate, and not misleading, consistent with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Act, applicable state law, and the advertising policies of the platforms we use, including Google Ads.

  • We identify ourselves as an independent authorized retailer, not as EarthLink itself.
  • Pricing, speeds, and terms are presented with the material conditions that apply, and we avoid “bait-and-switch” tactics.
  • “Up to” speeds and starting prices are labeled as such.

2. Clear disclosures

Where an offer has conditions (for example, term agreements, taxes and fees, or address-based availability), we disclose them clearly and near the claim they modify.

3. Platform & channel compliance

  • Search & display ads: we follow Google Ads and other network policies, including rules on trademarks, misrepresentation, and required disclosures.
  • Email: we follow the CAN-SPAM Act — accurate headers, a valid physical address, and a working unsubscribe link.
  • Calls & texts: we follow our TCPA Policy and honor do-not-call requests.

4. Testimonials & endorsements

Any testimonials reflect individual experiences and are used with permission or shown as illustrative. Results such as speed and price vary by customer and address.

5. Reporting a concern

If you believe an ad of ours is inaccurate or misleading, tell us at marketing@skyline-connect.example. We investigate and correct issues quickly.
