Payment security

PCI DSS Compliance

How we keep payment information safe and why you won't be typing card numbers into this site.

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Skyline Connect LLC, an authorized EarthLink retailer

Skyline Connect LLC is an independent authorized retailer of EarthLink services. We are not EarthLink LLC, and this is not the official EarthLink website.

1. Our commitment

We take payment security seriously and support handling of cardholder data in line with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), the framework created by the major card brands to protect payment information.

2. We minimize our exposure

  • This marketing website does not ask you to enter full payment card numbers.
  • Payment for EarthLink service is processed through EarthLink’s own secure, PCI DSS-compliant order and billing systems.
  • Where any payment step is handled, we use PCI DSS-compliant processors and, where possible, tokenization so sensitive card data never touches our servers.

3. Safeguards

  • Encryption of data in transit using industry-standard TLS.
  • Access to any sensitive systems restricted on a need-to-know basis.
  • Reputable, PCI-validated service providers for any payment handling.
  • Ongoing monitoring and vendor due diligence.

Never share full card numbers, PINs, or one-time passcodes by email or text. A legitimate representative will not ask you to.

4. Reporting a concern

If you suspect a payment security issue, contact us immediately at security@skyline-connect.example or (855) 555-0100.
