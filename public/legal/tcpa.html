TCPA Policy

How we handle phone and text contact, your consent, and how to opt out whenever you like.

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Skyline Connect LLC, an authorized EarthLink retailer

Skyline Connect LLC is an independent authorized retailer of EarthLink services. We are not EarthLink LLC, and this is not the official EarthLink website.

1. Your consent

When you submit a form or check the consent box on our website, you agree that Skyline Connect LLC and its authorized partners may contact you at the phone number you provided — including through automatic telephone dialing systems, prerecorded or artificial voice messages, and SMS/text messages — about EarthLink products, offers, and your request.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can still order service without agreeing to automated marketing contact — just call us at (855) 555-0100.

2. Message frequency & rates

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. We do not control carrier charges.

3. How to opt out

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for help.
  • Phone calls: tell the representative you wish to be placed on our internal do-not-call list.
  • Email or in writing: contact optout@skyline-connect.example with your name and number.

We honor opt-out requests promptly and maintain an internal do-not-call list. We also respect the National Do Not Call Registry.

4. Records

We keep records of the consent you provide, including the date, time, and the specific language you agreed to, consistent with applicable law.

5. Questions

For questions about this policy or our calling practices, contact our compliance team at compliance@skyline-connect.example.
