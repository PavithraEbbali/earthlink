After you order

Service Fulfillment Policy

What to expect from the moment you place an order through installation, billing, and support.

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Skyline Connect LLC, an authorized EarthLink retailer

Skyline Connect LLC is an independent authorized retailer of EarthLink services. We are not EarthLink LLC, and this is not the official EarthLink website.

1. Placing your order

After you check availability, a specialist confirms the plan, price, and terms for your service address. Your order is submitted to EarthLink for provisioning once you approve it. The service agreement for internet service is between you and EarthLink.

2. Installation & activation

  • Wireless Home Internet typically ships to your address for self-installation in about 15 minutes — place the gateway near a window and power it on.
  • Fiber usually requires a professional installation appointment; timing depends on your area.
  • Satellite may require professional installation of equipment.
  • Exact timelines, equipment, and any installation charges are confirmed before you order.

3. Billing

EarthLink bills you directly for your monthly service. Prices may exclude taxes, regulatory fees, and equipment charges. Promotional rates, if any, and their duration are disclosed at the time of order. We do not store your full payment card details on this website (see PCI DSS).

4. Changes, moves & upgrades

You can request plan changes, upgrades, or a move of service. We’ll help coordinate the request with EarthLink; availability and pricing at a new address may differ.

5. Cancellations & term agreements

  • Fiber and Satellite may include a 12-month term agreement; early cancellation could incur fees set by EarthLink.
  • Wireless Home Internet is offered with no annual contract.
  • Any applicable cancellation terms are presented to you before you order.

6. Getting help

For order status or help, contact us at (855) 555-0100 or support@skyline-connect.example. For technical support of active service, EarthLink’s support channels are also available to you as a customer.
