Privacy & Data Protection

This notice explains what we collect when you request a quote or order EarthLink service through us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Applies to Skyline Connect LLC, an authorized EarthLink retailer

We are an independent authorized retailer of EarthLink services. This policy covers information handled by Skyline Connect LLC. When you become an EarthLink customer, EarthLink’s own privacy policy also applies to the service it provides.

1. Information we collect

We only ask for what we need to check availability and prepare your order. Depending on how you interact with us, that may include:

  • Details you give us: your name, service address or ZIP, phone number, email, and the products you’re interested in.
  • Order information: the plan you select and information needed to complete your EarthLink order.
  • Automatic data: basic device and usage information such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, gathered through cookies and similar tools (see our Cookies Policy).

We do not ask you to enter full payment card numbers on this website. Payment is handled through EarthLink’s secure, PCI DSS-compliant order systems.

2. How we use your information

  • To confirm which EarthLink services are available at your address and prepare an accurate quote.
  • To contact you about your request, in line with your consent and our TCPA Policy.
  • To process and support the order you place with us.
  • To improve our website, measure marketing performance, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

3. When we share your information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as needed to serve you, including with:

  • EarthLink and its provisioning partners, to place and fulfill your order.
  • Service providers that help us run our business (for example, hosting, CRM, and call handling) under contracts that require them to protect your data.
  • Authorities where required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Some sharing for online advertising may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under certain state laws. You can opt out using the controls in Section 4 and our Cookies Policy.

4. Your privacy rights & choices

Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Virginia, and other U.S. states), you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale/sharing” of personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To make a request, contact us using Section 7. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by law. You can also opt out of marketing calls and texts at any time as described in our Marketing Policy.

5. How we protect your information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and vendor due diligence. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and to limit access to those who need it.

6. Data retention & children

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, then delete or de-identify it. Our website and services are intended for adults; we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
