Cookies Policy

What cookies are, how we use them, and how you stay in control.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as pixels, local storage, and tags. Together they help the site work, remember your choices, and measure how our marketing performs.

2. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function — for example, security, load balancing, and remembering items you enter in a form. These cannot be switched off in our systems.

Performance & analytics

Help us understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed) so we can improve it. They collect aggregated information.

Functional

Remember choices you make, such as your preferred options, to give you a smoother experience.

Advertising

Used by us and advertising partners (such as Google) to measure campaigns and show relevant ads on other sites. These may set cookies through our site.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where required and align our advertising cookies with our Privacy Policy and Marketing Policy.

3. Managing your cookies

  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Most browsers let you refuse new cookies and remove existing ones.
  • Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.
  • Where we show a consent banner, you can change your choices there at any time.

4. Opting out of advertising cookies

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through industry tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (optout.aboutads.info), the Network Advertising Initiative, and, for Google, its Ads Settings. State residents can also exercise the choices described in our Privacy Policy.
