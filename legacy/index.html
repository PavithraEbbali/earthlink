Good to know

Questions, answered

Is this the official EarthLink website?

No. Skyline Connect LLC is an independent, authorized retailer of EarthLink internet services. We help you compare and order EarthLink plans, while EarthLink LLC owns the brand and operates the underlying network.

What can I order through you?

EarthLink Fiber (100 Mbps to 5 Gig, symmetrical), Wireless 5G and fixed-wireless Home Internet, and Satellite internet for rural or hard-to-reach homes. What’s available depends on your service address, which we’ll check for you.

Does EarthLink Fiber have data caps?

Fiber plans include unlimited data with no overage fees. Wireless Home Internet plans include a monthly data allowance that varies by plan — we’ll walk you through the details before you order.

How quickly can I get connected?

Wireless Home Internet self-installs in roughly 15 minutes — place the gateway near a window and plug it in. Fiber usually needs a professional installation appointment. Exact timing depends on your address and product.

Is there a contract?

Fiber and Satellite generally use a 12-month term agreement, while Wireless Home Internet is offered with no annual contract. We confirm all terms with you up front — no surprises.

How do you protect my information?

We only use the details you share to prepare your quote and process your order, handled over secure, PCI DSS-aligned systems. Read our Privacy Policy and PCI DSS statement for specifics.
